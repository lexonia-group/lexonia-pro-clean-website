Switching cleaning companies takes one walkthrough, one written scope and a clear handover of access. The new company sees your space, sends a written scope and an exact price, you confirm it, and the first visit is a reset clean that brings everything up to standard. Two weeks later the scope is adjusted until it fits.

## Before you switch

- **Write down what was not working.** Missed rooms, a schedule that clashed with your hours, supplies running out. Each point belongs in the new written scope, so it is in writing.
- **Check your current agreement.** Note how much notice it asks for, so the last visit of one company and the first visit of the next line up.
- **List who needs access.** Codes, keys or badges, and anywhere the crew should not go.

## The changeover, step by step

1. **Ask for a quote.** Use the quote form, call or email. Every message gets a same-day response.
2. **Free walkthrough.** About 45 to 60 minutes at your space. We go over what needs to be cleaned and how often, including the things that were not working before.
3. **Written scope and exact price.** Within 24 hours. Cleaning liquids and equipment are included, with no surprise fees.
4. **You confirm** the scope and the schedule.
5. **Access.** Make sure access codes, keys or badges are current, and tell us about anything we should not touch.
6. **First visit: a reset clean.** It brings the space up to standard, evenings and nights, on your written checklist. A completed copy is left on site.
7. **Two-week check-in.** We check in and adjust the scope until it fits.

## What does not change

Your consumables stay where they are. Your company or building management supplies the trash bags, soap, paper towels and tissue, and we restock them from your supply every visit and tell you when something is running low.

## What to expect from us

Our crews are insured and background-checked, and we clean between 4 PM and 3 AM, seven days a week, when your business is closed. For a small office or store up to 10,000 sq ft, recurring cleaning is priced per visit by the size of the space, from $155 per visit for 1,000 to 2,000 sq ft, weekly, plus tax.

We clean offices and small retail stores across the Twin Cities and suburbs.
