After-hours cleaning happens in the evening or at night, after your business closes, so the cleaning never gets in the way of your team or your customers. Each visit follows the same written checklist, consumables are restocked from your own supply, and a completed copy of the checklist is left on site so you can see in the morning what was done.

## Why evenings and nights

- **Nobody is interrupted.** Vacuums, mopping and restroom cleaning happen when desks and meeting rooms are empty.
- **Floors get a chance to dry.** Mopped floors are dry before the first person walks in.
- **The space is ready at opening.** The team starts the day in a reset office, and customers walk into a clean reception area.

We clean between 4 PM and 3 AM, seven days a week, at the time agreed in your written scope.

## What happens at every visit

1. **We arrive after hours,** when your business is closed.
2. **We work the checklist:** restrooms, break rooms, workspaces and common areas. For retail: the sales floor, mirrors and fitting rooms.
3. **We restock** soap, paper towels, tissue and liners from your supply, and tell you when something is running low.
4. **We leave a completed checklist on site,** so you can see exactly what was done.

## How to prepare for the first visit

- Clear loose paper from desks where you can. We dust around whatever is left and never move, stack or read paperwork.
- Put confidential documents away.
- Make sure access codes, keys or badges are current.
- Flag anything we should not touch.

## The first weeks

The first visit is a reset clean that brings everything up to standard. Recurring visits keep it there. Two weeks in, we check in with you and adjust the scope until it fits how your office works.

Our crews are insured and background-checked. We clean offices and small retail stores across the Twin Cities and suburbs.
