It depends on how the space is used, not only on its size. For many small offices, a professional visit weekly or every other week covers restrooms, the break room, floors and high-touch points, while staff handle quick tidying in between. Offices with daily visitors or a busy break room usually need more.

## What decides the right frequency

Four things matter more than square footage:

- **People in the space each day.** More people means more trash, more dust on shared surfaces and more traffic on the floors.
- **Visitors and customers.** If clients walk through reception or a conference room, those areas need to look right every day they come in.
- **Restrooms and the break room.** These get used all day, and they are what people notice first. A busy break room sink or a shared restroom sets the schedule more than anything else.
- **Floors at the entry.** In a Minnesota winter, salt and slush come in on every pair of shoes, so entry areas and floors need regular attention.

## A simple way to think about it

- **Every other week** often fits a small office with a few people, few visitors and light kitchen use.
- **Weekly** fits most small offices with a full team in every day, a shared break room and regular visitors.
- **More often than weekly** fits offices with heavy daily traffic, customers on site every day, or a break room that is busy from morning to evening. Daily cleaning is usually set up as a flat monthly contract after a walkthrough.

## What happens between visits

Between professional visits, the everyday basics stay with the team: rinsing their own dishes, wiping up a spill, keeping papers off shared tables. The professional visit then resets the restrooms, break room, floors and high-touch points to the same standard every time.
