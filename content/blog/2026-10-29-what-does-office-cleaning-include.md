A recurring office cleaning visit covers the restrooms, the break room, workspaces, common areas and high-touch points, on a written checklist. It does not cover moving furniture or paperwork, operating your machines, or biohazard, hoarding and post-construction cleanup. The cleaning company brings its cleaning liquids and equipment; your company or building supplies the consumables.

## Room by room

These are the items on our written office checklist. A completed copy is left on site after every visit.

### Restrooms

- Toilets and urinals disinfected
- Sinks, counters and fixtures
- Mirrors
- Partitions, stall doors and handles
- Floors swept and mopped with disinfectant
- Trash emptied and bins re-lined
- Soap, towels, tissue and liners restocked from your supply

### Break room or kitchenette

- Counters and backsplash wiped down
- Sink scrubbed and rinsed
- Appliance exteriors: fridge, microwave, dishwasher
- Tables and chairs
- Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
- Trash emptied, floor vacuumed and mopped

### Offices and workspaces

- Desks and accessible surfaces dusted, around whatever is on them
- Shelves, sills and ledges
- Trash emptied at every station and re-lined
- Carpets vacuumed and hard floors mopped

### Common areas

- Reception, lobby and waiting areas
- Conference rooms: tables wiped, chairs squared
- Interior glass on doors and partitions
- Corridors, entry mats, and stair and elevator landings within the suite

### High-touch points

Door handles and push plates, light switches, elevator and keypad buttons, handrails, shared appliance handles and the reception counter edge are disinfected every visit.

## What is not included

- Windows (interior windows can be added to a visit)
- Operating or maintaining your machines
- Moving furniture, equipment or paperwork
- Biohazard, hoarding or post-construction cleanup

Papers stay where you left them: we never move, stack or read paperwork, and nothing is opened, filed, thrown out or rearranged.

## Who supplies what

We bring the cleaning liquids and our own equipment, all included in the price. Your company or building management supplies the consumables: trash bags, soap, paper towels, tissue and restroom disposal bags. We restock them from your supply every visit and tell you when something is running low.

We clean offices and small retail stores across the Twin Cities and suburbs, evenings and nights.
