A free cleaning walkthrough takes about 45 to 60 minutes at your space. We go over what needs to be cleaned and how often, and within 24 hours you get a written scope and an exact price. The exact price comes from what we see at the walkthrough.

## What we look at

- **Every room on the checklist.** Restrooms, the break room or kitchenette, workspaces, common areas and conference rooms. In a store: the sales floor, fitting rooms, mirrors, counters and back rooms.
- **How the space is used.** How many people work there each day, whether customers or visitors come in, and how much the restrooms and break room get used. That is what decides how often a space needs cleaning.
- **The size of the space.** Recurring cleaning up to 10,000 sq ft is priced per visit by size.
- **Your hours.** We clean between 4 PM and 3 AM, seven days a week, after your business closes, so we agree the evenings that fit.
- **Access.** Codes, keys or badges, and anything we should not touch.
- **Add-ons.** Interior windows are not on the standard checklist but can be added to a visit: $40 for 1 to 5, $70 for 6 to 10, $110 for 11 to 15, before sales tax.

## What to have ready

- The approximate square footage, if you know it
- How often you would like the space cleaned
- The days and times your business is closed
- Anything that has bothered you about cleaning before

## What you get within 24 hours

A written scope and an exact price. The scope lists the rooms and tasks, how often, and what is not included. The price is before sales tax, includes the cleaning liquids and our equipment, and has no travel charges or surprise fees.

- Weekly or every other week, up to 10,000 sq ft: a set price per visit, from $155 for 1,000 to 2,000 sq ft, weekly, plus tax.
- Daily cleaning, or a space over 10,000 sq ft: a flat monthly contract.
- Multi-story buildings: custom pricing, at greater discounts.

## After you say yes

The first visit is a reset clean that brings everything up to standard, and a completed copy of the checklist is left on site. Two weeks in, we check in with you and adjust the scope until it fits.

We clean offices and small retail stores across the Twin Cities and suburbs.
