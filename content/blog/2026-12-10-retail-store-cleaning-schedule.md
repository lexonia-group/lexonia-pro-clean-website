A small retail store or boutique is cleaned after it closes, so the work never meets a customer. Visits happen in the evening or at night, weekly or every other week, on a checklist built around the store, with a completed copy left on site so you can see in the morning what was done.

## Why after close

- **No customers in the way.** The sales floor, fitting rooms and restroom are cleaned while the store is empty.
- **Floors dry before opening.** The sales floor is swept and mopped, so it is dry when the first customer walks in.
- **The store opens ready.** Mirrors, counters and fitting rooms are clean at the start of the day.

We clean between 4 PM and 3 AM, seven days a week, so a visit can start after your last customer leaves.

## What gets cleaned in a store

- Sales floor swept and mopped, rugs vacuumed
- Mirrors cleaned and display pieces wiped down
- Front counter wiped, counter edge disinfected
- Fitting rooms wiped down
- Restroom, break room and back rooms
- Trash emptied and bins re-lined

Restroom soap, towels, tissue and liners are restocked from your own supply.

## How often

It depends on how the store is used: how many customers come through and how much the restroom and back rooms get used. Recurring visits are weekly or every other week. Recurring cleaning up to 10,000 sq ft is priced per visit by the size of the space, from $155 per visit for 1,000 to 2,000 sq ft, weekly, plus tax. Daily cleaning, or a store over 10,000 sq ft, is a flat monthly contract quoted after a free walkthrough.

## Getting the schedule right

1. **Free walkthrough.** About 45 to 60 minutes. We go over the store, your hours and what needs to be cleaned.
2. **Written scope and exact price,** within 24 hours.
3. **First visit: a reset clean** that brings the store up to standard.
4. **Two-week check-in.** We adjust the scope until it fits how your store works.

Make sure access codes or keys are current, and tell us about anything we should not touch, such as stock or displays.
