In a multi-story building, cleaning can rotate floor by floor: a different floor, or two floors, each night. The whole building is cleaned once every one to two weeks, depending on what it needs, and every visit follows a written checklist.

## How the rotation works

Each night covers one floor, or two. As an illustration, a five-floor building cleaned one floor a night comes around in five nights; cleaned two floors a night, it comes around faster. How often each floor comes around depends on what your building needs.

## What decides the pace

- **How each floor is used.** Floors with more people, visitors or shared restrooms and break rooms need to come around more often.
- **The size of each floor.** Some buildings can take two floors a night.
- **Your hours.** We clean between 4 PM and 3 AM, seven days a week, when your business is closed.

## What every visit includes

Each floor is cleaned on the written checklist: restrooms, break rooms, workspaces, common areas and high-touch points. A completed copy of the checklist is left on site. Consumables come from your company or building management, and we restock them from that supply and say when something is running low.

## How it is priced

Multi-story buildings get custom pricing, at greater discounts. The price follows a free walkthrough of about 45 to 60 minutes, and within 24 hours you get a written scope and an exact price, with the cleaning liquids and our equipment included.

## Common questions

- **How is the pace set?** At the walkthrough we go over how each floor is used and agree how often it comes around; the two-week check-in adjusts it.
- **Who supplies trash bags, soap and paper towels?** Your company or building management. We do not supply or buy them; we restock from your supply every visit.
- **Do you clean windows?** Interior windows are not on the standard checklist but can be added to a visit.
- **Are you a franchise?** No. Lexonia Pro Clean LLC is a locally owned company based in Eden Prairie, Minnesota.
