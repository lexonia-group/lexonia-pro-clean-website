For a small office or store up to 10,000 sq ft, recurring cleaning is priced per visit by the size of the space, weekly or every other week. With us it starts from $155 per visit for 1,000 to 2,000 sq ft, weekly, plus tax. Daily cleaning, or a space over 10,000 sq ft, is a flat monthly contract quoted after a walkthrough.

## What sets the price

- **Size of the space.** The per-visit price steps up with square footage, from 1,000 up to 10,000 sq ft.
- **How often.** Weekly or every other week. The schedule is part of the written scope, so the price you see is the price for your frequency.
- **Add-ons.** Interior windows are not on the standard checklist. They can be added to a visit, priced by the number of windows: $40 for 1 to 5, $70 for 6 to 10, $110 for 11 to 15, before sales tax.

## What is already in the price

- The cleaning liquids and our own equipment
- The full written checklist, with a completed copy left on site every visit
- No travel charges and no surprise fees

Consumables such as trash bags, soap, paper towels and tissue are not in the price: your company or building management supplies them, and we restock them from your supply.

## When a monthly contract fits better

- **Daily cleaning**, or more than one visit a week
- **Spaces over 10,000 sq ft**
- **Multi-story buildings**, where we clean a different floor, or two, each night so the whole building is covered every one to two weeks. These get custom pricing, at greater discounts.

In each case the monthly price is quoted after the walkthrough, so you know the exact cost before the first visit.

## How to get your exact price

Book a free walkthrough, about 45 to 60 minutes. Within 24 hours you get a written scope and an exact price. Prices are before sales tax.

We clean offices and small retail stores across the Twin Cities and suburbs, evenings and nights.
