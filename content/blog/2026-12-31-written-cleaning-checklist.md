A written cleaning checklist turns "the office gets cleaned" into a list everyone can see: which rooms, which tasks, every visit. With a completed copy left on site after each visit, you can see in the morning exactly what was done, without having to go looking.

## What it should cover

- **Restrooms:** toilets and urinals disinfected, sinks, counters and fixtures, mirrors, partitions and handles, floors swept and mopped, trash emptied, supplies restocked from your stock.
- **Break room or kitchenette:** counters and backsplash, the sink, appliance exteriors, tables and chairs, cabinet fronts, trash and floors.
- **Workspaces:** desks and accessible surfaces dusted around whatever is on them, shelves, sills and ledges, trash at every station, carpets vacuumed and hard floors mopped.
- **Common areas:** reception, conference rooms, interior glass on doors and partitions, corridors and entry mats.
- **High-touch points:** door handles, light switches, elevator and keypad buttons, handrails and shared appliance handles.

In a store, the checklist is built around the store: sales floor, mirrors, display pieces, the front counter and fitting rooms.

## What it should say is not included

Windows (interior windows can be added to a visit), moving furniture, equipment or paperwork, operating your machines, and biohazard, hoarding or post-construction cleanup.

## Why the completed copy matters

- **You can see what was done.** The completed checklist stays with you after every visit.
- **The scope can be tuned.** Two weeks after the first clean, we check in and adjust the scope until it fits how your space is used.
- **Everyone works from the same list,** visit after visit.

## Common questions

- **Is the checklist the same for every client?** The office checklist is our standard, and the walkthrough and two-week check-in adjust it to your space. Retail checklists are built around the store.
- **Do you move papers to clean desks?** No. Desks and accessible surfaces are dusted around whatever is on them; we never move, stack or read paperwork.
- **When do you clean?** Evenings and nights, between 4 PM and 3 AM, seven days a week, when your business is closed.
