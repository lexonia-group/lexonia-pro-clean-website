Compare cleaning proposals by what is written down, not by the headline price. A useful proposal lists every room and what is done in it, how often and when, the exact price and what it covers, who supplies the consumables, and what is not included. When two proposals do not list the same things, their prices are not comparable.

## What a written scope should list

- **Rooms and areas.** Restrooms, the break room, workspaces, common areas and conference rooms. For a store: the sales floor, fitting rooms, mirrors, counters and back rooms.
- **The tasks in each room.** For example: toilets and urinals disinfected, counters and sinks wiped, trash emptied and bins re-lined, carpets vacuumed and hard floors mopped, high-touch points such as door handles and light switches disinfected.
- **How often, and when.** Weekly, every other week, or more often, and on which evenings. We clean between 4 PM and 3 AM, after your business closes.
- **The price, and what is in it.** Per visit or per month, before sales tax. Ours includes the cleaning liquids and our own equipment, with no travel charges and no surprise fees.
- **Who supplies what.** With us, your company or building management supplies the consumables: trash bags, soap, paper towels, tissue and restroom disposal bags. We restock them from your supply every visit.
- **What is not included.** Windows (interior windows can be added to a visit), moving furniture, equipment or paperwork, operating your machines, and biohazard, hoarding or post-construction cleanup.

## Questions worth asking every company

1. **Is the price exact, or an estimate?** Ours is an exact price, in writing, within 24 hours of a free walkthrough.
2. **How will I know what was done?** We leave a completed copy of the checklist on site after every visit.
3. **What happens in the first weeks?** Our first visit is a reset clean that brings everything up to standard. Two weeks in, we check in with you and adjust the scope until it fits.
4. **Are the crews insured and background-checked?** Ours are. The certificate of insurance is available on request.
5. **Who are we dealing with?** Lexonia Pro Clean LLC is a locally owned company based in Eden Prairie, not a franchise.

## How our pricing reads on paper

For a small office or store up to 10,000 sq ft, recurring cleaning is priced per visit by the size of the space, weekly or every other week. It starts from $155 per visit for 1,000 to 2,000 sq ft, weekly, plus tax. Daily cleaning, or a space over 10,000 sq ft, is a flat monthly contract quoted after the walkthrough. Multi-story buildings get custom pricing, at greater discounts.
