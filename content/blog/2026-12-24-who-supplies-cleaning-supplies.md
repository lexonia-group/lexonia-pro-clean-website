With us, your company or building management supplies the consumables: trash bags, soap, paper towels, tissue and restroom disposal bags. We bring the cleaning liquids and our own equipment, all included in the price. We restock the consumables from your supply every visit and tell you when something is running low.

## Who brings what

- **We bring:** the cleaning liquids and our own equipment, included in the price.
- **You supply:** trash bags, soap, paper towels, tissue and restroom disposal bags. We do not supply or buy them.

Because consumables stay with you, there is no supply line on our invoice: the price is the cleaning, with no travel charges and no surprise fees.

## How restocking works

At every visit, restroom soap, towels, tissue and liners are restocked from your supply, and trash bins are emptied and re-lined. When something is running low, we tell you, so you can reorder before it runs out.

## What to keep in stock

- Trash bags for every bin size you use
- Hand soap for restrooms and the break room
- Paper towels and toilet tissue
- Restroom disposal bags

Keep them where the crew can reach them after hours, and tell us where they are at the walkthrough.

## Common questions

- **Can you buy the supplies for us?** No. We do not supply or buy consumables; they come from your company or building management, and we restock from that supply.
- **What if something runs out between visits?** We tell you when something is running low, so you can reorder in time.
- **Are cleaning liquids extra?** No. The cleaning liquids and our own equipment are included in the price.
- **Does this change for a store or a multi-story building?** No. The same split applies to offices, small retail stores and larger buildings.

## Where it is written down

Who supplies what is part of your written scope, alongside the rooms, tasks, schedule and price. The scope and an exact price arrive within 24 hours of a free walkthrough of about 45 to 60 minutes.
