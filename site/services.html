The Checklist

What We Do Every Visit

Office visits follow this written scope, and a completed copy is left on site. Retail checklists are built around your store.

Restrooms

  • Toilets and urinals disinfected
  • Sinks, counters and fixtures
  • Mirrors
  • Partitions, stall doors and handles
  • Floors swept and mopped with disinfectant
  • Trash emptied and bins re-lined
  • Soap, towels, tissue and liners restocked from your supply

Break room / kitchenette

  • Counters and backsplash wiped down
  • Sink scrubbed and rinsed
  • Appliance exteriors — fridge, microwave, dishwasher
  • Tables and chairs
  • Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
  • Trash emptied and bins re-lined
  • Floor vacuumed and mopped

Offices and workspaces

  • Desks and accessible surfaces dusted
  • Shelves, sills and ledges
  • Trash emptied at every station and re-lined
  • Carpets vacuumed
  • Hard floors mopped

Common areas

  • Reception, lobby and waiting areas
  • Conference rooms — tables wiped, chairs squared
  • Interior glass on doors and partitions
  • Corridors vacuumed and mopped
  • Stair and elevator landings within the suite
  • Entry mats vacuumed or shaken out

High-touch points, disinfected

  • Door handles and push plates
  • Light switches
  • Elevator and keypad buttons
  • Handrails
  • Shared appliance handles and controls
  • Reception counter edge

Small retail stores

  • Sales floor swept and mopped
  • Rugs vacuumed
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • Display pieces wiped down
  • Front counter wiped, counter edge disinfected
  • Fitting rooms wiped down
  • Restroom, break room, back rooms and trash

Not included, so there are no surprises

  • Windows (interior windows can be added)
  • Operating or maintaining your machines
  • Consumables such as trash bags, soap, paper towels and tissue — your company or building management supplies them
  • Moving furniture, equipment or paperwork
  • Biohazard, hoarding or post-construction cleanup

Your company or building management supplies the consumables: trash bags, soap, paper towels, tissue and restroom disposal bags. We do not supply or buy them. We bring the cleaning liquids and we restock the consumables from your supply every visit and tell you when something is running low.
