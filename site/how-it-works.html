FAQ

Common Questions

What does Lexonia Pro Clean clean?

We clean offices and small retail stores and boutiques across the Twin Cities metro: workspaces, common areas, conference rooms, break rooms, restrooms, sales floors, mirrors, fitting rooms and back rooms. Every visit follows a written checklist.

Where is Lexonia Pro Clean based, and where do you work?

We are based in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, and serve the Twin Cities metro. If you are just outside it, ask anyway.

When do you clean?

Evenings and nights, when your business is closed, so we are never in your way.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring the cleaning liquids and our own equipment. All of it is included in the price, with no surprise fees and no travel charges.

Who supplies trash bags, soap and paper towels?

Your company or building management supplies the consumables: trash bags, soap, paper towels, tissue and restroom disposal bags. We do not supply or buy them. We restock them from your supply every visit and tell you when something is running low.

How does pricing work?

We start with a free walkthrough of your space, about 45 to 60 minutes. Within 24 hours you get a written scope and an exact price. For spaces up to 10,000 sq ft, weekly or every other week, the price is per visit by size of space (see the price card on the Services page), before sales tax. Daily cleaning, or a space over 10,000 sq ft, is a flat monthly contract quoted after the walkthrough, so you know your exact cost before we start. Multi-story buildings get custom pricing, at greater discounts.

How fast do you respond to a quote request?

Every message gets a same-day response.

What happens after the first visit?

The first visit is a reset clean that brings everything up to standard. Recurring visits then maintain it from there. We check in after two weeks and adjust the scope until it fits.

How will I know what was cleaned?

Every visit follows a written checklist, and a completed copy is left on site so you can see exactly what was done.

Are you insured, and are your crews background-checked?

Yes. Lexonia Pro Clean is fully insured, and our crews are background-checked.

Can you clean a multi-story building?

Yes. We can clean a different floor, or two floors, each night, so the whole building is cleaned once every 1-2 weeks, depending on what it needs. Multi-story buildings get custom pricing, at greater discounts.

Do you clean windows?

Interior windows can be added to a visit, priced by the number of windows (1-5, 6-10 or 11-15), before sales tax. They are not part of the standard checklist.

Will you move or read the papers on my desk?

No. Desks are dusted around whatever is on them. We never move, stack or read paperwork, and nothing is opened, filed, thrown out or rearranged.

What is not included?

Operating or maintaining your machines, moving furniture, equipment or paperwork, and biohazard, hoarding or post-construction cleanup.
